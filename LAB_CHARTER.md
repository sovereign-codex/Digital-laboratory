# Digital Laboratory Charter

## Purpose

The Digital Laboratory is the durable evidence layer for reality-facing research. Temporary computational workspaces may be used as a bench, but the durable record lives here.

## Surface separation

- Temporary bench: exploratory computation and transient analysis.
- Digital-laboratory repository: versioned experiment records, evidence references, analysis, and dispositions.
- Notion Laboratory: project-level hypothesis state, maturity, ownership, relationships, and next experiment.
- Tyme-Lab: institutional/runtime contract experiments; separate from scientific and engineering experiments.
- TYME Hall Laboratory: selected public findings after review.

## State law

PROPOSED != AUTHORIZED != EXECUTED != VERIFIED != REPLICATED

A design is not a run. A run is not a verified result. A verified result is not a replication. Simulation and synthetic validation do not count as physical evidence.

## Execution classes

DESIGN_ONLY, SYNTHETIC, SIMULATION, PHYSICAL, FIELD.

## Evidence states

NONE, THREAD_BOUND, PARTIAL, VERIFIED, REPLICATED, INVALID.

## Recovery rule

Historical work is admitted conservatively. Memory or conversation history can establish lineage but must not be upgraded to executed or verified without surviving evidence. Unknown is preferable to invented continuity.

## Evidence custody

A run should preserve identity, question, execution class, controls, inputs, environment, observations, analysis, contradictions, disposition, provenance, and justified next action. Summaries may index evidence but must not replace it.
